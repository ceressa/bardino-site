Privacy on bardino.app

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This page is about this website, bardino.app. Each game has its own privacy policy: Decimo, Colmo and Palmo.

What this site does not do

It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising. It has no forms and no accounts. It loads no fonts, scripts or images from other servers. We do not collect or store anything about visitors ourselves.

Hosting

The site is published with GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. Like any web server, they receive your IP address and browser details with each request and may keep them in their logs to deliver and protect the site. Their own policies apply: GitHub, Cloudflare.

E-mail

If you write to info@bardino.app, we use your address and your message only to reply to you.
